## 1. The Commitments

I commit to completing each habit below **on or before its deadline**, every day.

{{commitments_table}}

## 2. What Counts as Success

- A habit is **done** only if it is checked off **on or before its deadline**.
- A habit that is unchecked when its deadline passes is **failed**.
- A habit checked off **after** its deadline is also **failed**. Late is not done.
- A failed day stays failed. No retroactive edits to the log.

## 3. The Stakes

Each failure triggers the penalty attached to that habit, automatically and without negotiation.

{{stakes_table}}

## 4. Serving a Penalty

- A penalty must be completed within **24 hours** of being triggered.
- Once completed, it is marked as served in the tracker (`fulfill <id>`).
- A penalty not served within 24 hours is **doubled**.

## 5. Exceptions

- Only these excuses are valid: **illness, emergency, pre-announced travel**.
- An exception must be declared **before** the deadline, never after.
- An excuse invented after a failure does not cancel the penalty.

## 6. Amendments

- The terms above can only be changed on the review date.
- No mid-week renegotiation, and no "just this once".
